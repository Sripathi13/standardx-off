# STANDARD X - BIS & IRC Standards Reference Guide

## 1. Statutory Role of Indian Engineering Codes
In India, civil works under government bodies (CPWD, NHAI, NBCC, State PWDs) and private contracts governed by RERA must strictly adhere to the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications.

## 2. Core Material Standards Catalog

### 2.1 Cement & Pozzolanic Binders

#### IS 269:2015 - Ordinary Portland Cement (33, 43, and 53 Grades)
- **Mandatory Requirements**:
  - Compressive Strength (28 days): $\ge 53\text{ MPa}$ for Grade 53; $\ge 43\text{ MPa}$ for Grade 43
  - Blaine Fineness: Not less than $225\text{ m}^2/\text{kg}$
  - Setting Times: Initial $\ge 30\text{ min}$; Final $\le 600\text{ min}$
  - Soundness (Le-Chatelier): $\le 10\text{ mm}$ expansion
- **Key Test Methods**: Compressive Strength (IS 4031 Part 6), Blaine Fineness (IS 4031 Part 2), Soundness (IS 4031 Part 3).

#### IS 1489 (Part 1):2015 - Portland Pozzolana Cement (Fly Ash Based)
- **Mandatory Requirements**:
  - Reactive Fly Ash Replacement: $15\%$ to $35\%$ by mass conforming to IS 3812
  - 28-day Compressive Strength: $\ge 33\text{ MPa}$
  - Drying Shrinkage: $\le 0.15\%$
- **Applications**: Mass concrete foundations, hydraulic structures, exterior masonry plaster, residential slabs in coastal zones.

### 2.2 Concrete Reinforcement

#### IS 1786:2008 - High Strength Deformed Steel Bars and Wires
- **Grades Supported**: Fe 415, Fe 500, Fe 500D, Fe 550, Fe 550D
- **Mandatory Requirements**:
  - 0.2% Proof Stress: Minimum $500\text{ MPa}$ (Fe 500D) / $550\text{ MPa}$ (Fe 550D)
  - Elongation at Fracture: Minimum $16.0\%$ (Fe 500D) / $14.5\%$ (Fe 550D)
  - Tensile / Yield Ratio: $\ge 1.10$ for high energy absorption in seismic zones III, IV, and V
  - Mandrel Bend & Rebend Test: 180° cold bend without rupture or micro-cracking

---

### 2.3 Aggregates & Fine Sand

#### IS 383:2016 - Coarse and Fine Aggregate for Concrete
- **Fine Aggregate Classification**:
  - Zone I (Coarser sand for rich mixes)
  - Zone II (Universal structural grade for RCC)
  - Zone III / IV (Finer sand for plastering and finish)
- **Manufactured Sand (M-Sand)**: Hydro-washed vertical shaft impactor (VSI) crushed granite conforming to Zone II grading.
- **Coarse Aggregate Mechanical Limits**:
  - Aggregate Impact Value: Maximum $30\%$ for general concrete; maximum $18\%$ for bridge decks and highway pavements
  - Flakiness & Elongation Index: Combined maximum $35\%$

---

### 2.4 Masonry Units

#### IS 1077:1992 - Common Burnt Clay Bricks
- **Strength Classes**: Class 3.5, 5.0, 7.5, 10.0, 15.0 N/mm²
- **Water Absorption Limit**: Maximum $20\%$ by mass for Class 12.5 and below; $15\%$ for higher classes.

#### IS 2185 (Part 3):2008 - Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) Blocks
- **Grade 1 (Load Bearing & Infill)**:
  - Oven-dry density: $551$ to $650\text{ kg/m}^3$
  - Minimum compressive strength: $4.0\text{ N/mm}^2$
  - Thermal conductivity: $k \le 0.24\text{ W/m}\cdot\text{K}$

---

### 2.5 Highway & Pavement Standards

#### IS 73:2013 - Paving Bitumen (Viscosity Grade)
- **Grades**: VG-10, VG-20, VG-30, VG-40
- **VG-30 Benchmark Requirements**:
  - Absolute Viscosity at 60°C: $2400$ to $3600\text{ Poise}$
  - Penetration at 25°C: $45$ to $70\text{ (0.1 mm)}$
  - Softening Point: Minimum $47^\circ\text{C}$
  - Ductility at 25°C: Minimum $40\text{ cm}$
